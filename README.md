# DEM-KG

[Open DEM-KG](https://faisal-1921.github.io/DEM-KG/) · [References](https://faisal-1921.github.io/DEM-KG/references.html)

Published by EI LAB, Department of Electrical Engineering, Tsinghua University & ECE, The University of Hong Kong. Building a unified knowledge structure for DEM market design.

A rule knowledge graph with 338 current nodes, 477 S bundles, 525 visible rule slices and 201 clusters. Scientific definitions, mathematics, conditions and citations are accessible inline. Node, directed-edge, rule and cluster details link to the paper title, DOI and the corresponding numbered reference. The References page lists all 50 papers with authors, publication details and original-article links.

Hover over a layer abbreviation for its full name, or click it to open the eight-layer key. Review-status tags are omitted from the presentation; scientific scope, source conditions and substantive limitations remain available.

The graph opens at 100% scale. Select a market template to highlight its nodes and internal edges. Click a highlighted node to focus its incoming/outgoing edges and endpoints. Gray elements remain noninteractive context. Selecting a template, node, edge or layer does not change the viewport. Fit view is explicit. Rule-Chain and Rule-Cluster slices retain the full gray graph background.

No download controls or standalone Sources browser. Original Source documents, complete graph exports, translation maps and private audit records are excluded. Web code and browser-visible content remain copyable.
